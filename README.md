# 🛍️ Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard | Retail Sales Analysis

An interactive **Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, transactions, customers, products, regions, sales channels, and monthly sales trends.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of business performance and helps identify important patterns and trends in retail sales data.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard** transforms raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights using interactive Power BI visualizations.

The dashboard focuses on:

- 💰 Revenue & Sales Performance
- 🧾 Transaction Analysis
- 👥 Customer Analysis
- 📦 Product Performance
- 🌎 Regional Sales
- 🛒 Online vs Offline Sales
- 📅 Monthly Sales Trends

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance by Region

A horizontal bar chart shows total sales across different regions:

- South – ₹157M
- West – ₹157M
- East – ₹149M
- North – ₹145M

This helps compare regional sales performance.

---

### 2. Product Sales Analysis

The dashboard analyzes sales by product category/product name.

Top-performing products include:

- 💻 Laptop – ₹154M
- 📷 Camera – ₹111M
- 📱 Smartphone – ₹82M
- 📲 Tablet – ₹59M
- 🖥️ Monitor – ₹42M

---

### 3. Sales Channel Analysis

A donut chart compares sales between:

- 🟦 Online
- 🟨 Offline

This provides insights into customer purchasing channels and helps understand the contribution of online and offline sales.

---

### 4. Monthly Sales Trend

The line chart represents the monthly sales trend and allows users to compare sales performance across different months.

This helps identify:

- High-performing months
- Low-performing months
- Overall sales movement
- Seasonal sales patterns

---
